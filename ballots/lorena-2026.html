Practice ballot. This page is a planning aid and cannot be cast. Texas has no straight-ticket option, so every race is marked on its own.
Vote for the candidate of your choice in each race by placing an “X” or filling in the shape beside the candidate’s name. Vote por el candidato de su preferencia en cada candidatura marcando con una “X” o rellenando la forma junto al nombre del candidato.
The city of Lorena sits in voting precinct 75: Commissioner Precinct 1, Constable Precinct 1, State House District 56. Some rural Lorena addresses fall in neighboring voting precincts that use Commissioner Precinct 4 or Constable Precinct 5. Your voter registration card prints your precinct, and mclennan.gov/elections posts the sample ballot for each one.

Candidates. Federal, state, district and county races come from the Secretary of State's 2026 Ballot Certification (signed August 28, 2026), McLennan County section. Within each race the parties appear in the order the certification and the state sample ballot use: Republican, Democratic, Libertarian, Green. Unopposed candidates still appear on Texas ballots.

Write-in lines appear only where a declared write-in candidate qualified. The Secretary of State's certified write-in list puts them in the races for U.S. Senator, Governor and Lieutenant Governor. The county keeps its own write-in list for county offices; I could not open it.

Special election. Governor Abbott's August 18 proclamation orders a special election in Senate District 22 to serve out Brian Birdwell's unexpired term after his resignation. The regular race for the next full term is a separate contest on the same ballot. The special-election names and party labels come from the Associated Press race listing published by the Texas Tribune, because the state's special-election candidate page blocks automated access.

Precincts. Lorena's commissioner, constable and House assignments come from McLennan County's 2025 precinct layers, which the county supplied to the City of Waco GIS service in October 2025. Commissioner Precinct 1 is not up in 2026; only Precincts 2 and 4 are, and no Lorena-area voting precinct lies in Precinct 2.

No local propositions found. Lorena ISD elects trustees in May, and its board page lists no November 2026 election. The City of Lorena's adopted 2026 rate raises maintenance and operations revenue by 3.5%, which does not exceed the voter-approval rate for cities, so it triggers no automatic tax election, and the city site lists no November order of election. Texas has no constitutional amendments on this ballot; the Legislature sent its 17 to voters in November 2025.
